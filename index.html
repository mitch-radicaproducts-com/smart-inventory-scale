Connection

RS-232 on this model

  1. Use a USB-to-RS232 adapter, preferably FTDI, and a straight DB9 cable. Pin 2, pin 3, and pin 5 carry transmit, receive, and ground. The counting-scale manual does not draw the connector. The same seller’s other balances transmit on pin 2 and receive on pin 3.
  2. On the scale, press 7, 8, 9. The display cycles to diu. Press PCS/SET until bud, choose 9600, then press Alarm. Press PCS/SET until odE, press Alarm, then choose the output mode and press Alarm again. Turn the scale off and on.
  3. Output mode 1 sends when you press P. Mode 2 sends once the weight is stable. Mode 3 sends continuously.
  4. Open this page in Chrome on the Chromebook at http://localhost or an https address. Choose the USB serial port. The family default is 9600 baud, 8 data bits, no parity, 1 stop bit.

The manual’s terminal steps say to enter R, and they name a CAL/PCS key that this model does not have. The print key here is P. Send writes R, the request documented on the related HC balances, plus a carriage return when End with Enter is checked. The manual does not publish the print text, so every received line stays in the log. The cards fill in when a line names a weight, a piece count, or a unit weight.
